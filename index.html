A recipe from my grandmother's kitchen in Lodz

My grandmother rolled her babka dough on a floured tea towel and never once measured the cocoa. I spent close to a decade trying to get the ratio right before I opened this kitchen in 2019.

Babka & Co. now bakes four days a week out of a small storefront on Birchwood Lane, using the same braid-and-roll method and, honestly, a lot more butter than anyone should probably admit to.

We measure flour by feel as much as by weight, because dough behaves differently depending on the weather, and we keep our batches small so every loaf comes out the way it's supposed to.

How a Loaf Comes Together

Three days, start to finish. Nothing rushed, nothing frozen.

01

Overnight Rise

Dough gets mixed the evening before and rests slow in the cooler so the gluten develops without any rush.

02

Rolled and Swirled

We roll each batch by hand, layer in dark chocolate and toasted walnuts, then twist and braid the loaf.
